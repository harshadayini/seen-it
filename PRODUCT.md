# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Shoppers and hackathon judges watching a live demo on a phone. Someone sees a product in a video or photo (a lamp in a room tour) and wants the exact item, fast, with honest confidence about whether the match is right. In the demo, judges hand us unrehearsed videos and expect results to appear while they watch.

## Product Purpose

Seen It turns any video or photo into a shoppable list. It finds each item, matches it to real Shopify products with calibrated confidence, previews the product in the user's own scene, and hands off to each merchant's checkout. Success: a judge pastes a link, items appear within seconds, one recovers on a second look, one is honestly marked "no match", and the cart lands on a real merchant checkout. Nothing is ever charged in the app.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, calm, precise. A quiet gallery for everyday objects: confident serif headlines, generous space, small honest labels, one restrained accent. Voice is short and plain. It says "Exact", "Similar", "No match", never jargon.

## Anti-references

- A dashboard or telemetry look: no session IDs, protocol handshakes, tiny jargon labels, metric tiles.
- Cream/beige and purple-gradient "AI app" palettes.
- Glass blur, glow, and sparkle "AI" icons.
- Anything that reads as a chatbot or a text box first.

## Design Principles

1. **Honest over impressive.** Confidence and "no match" are shown plainly; nothing is dressed up as a result.
2. **Less words, more object.** The product and the footage lead. Every label earns its place; cut the rest.
3. **One quiet accent.** Neutral stone and ink carry the surface. Colour appears only where a decision is made.
4. **Phone first, thumb first.** Large targets, one primary action per screen, content never hidden behind fixed bars.
5. **Motion explains.** Items arriving, a match upgrading, a sheet opening. Nothing moves just to move.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, 44px minimum touch targets, full keyboard and screen reader support, and reduced-motion alternatives for every animation. Meaning is never carried by colour alone (status pills always carry words).
